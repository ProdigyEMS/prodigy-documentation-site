---

If your organization tracks requirements in Prodigy, your dashboard has a **My requirements** panel. It lists what your organization expects of you, what is due, and whom to ask about each item. {% .lead %}

You see your own items only. Nobody else's list is visible to you.

---

## Read the panel

Items are listed with the most urgent first. Each one shows:

- its name;
- a line saying how it gets done and whom to ask, such as "A training officer signs it off · ask Tara Officer";
- a status;
- a due date, when it has one.

When nothing is due, the panel reads "You're all set. Nothing is due right now."

## Clear an item

| The item is | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A certification that is expired, expiring or missing | Click **Update certifications** and add or update the certification with its new expiration date |
| A National Registry certification | Click **Verify with National Registry** on its row. If a date of birth is asked for, add it on your account profile. |
| A class | If it has been assigned to you, open **Assignments** and take it there. The panel links to your assignments. Otherwise ask the person named on the line. |
| Something a training officer signs off | Ask the person named on the line. You cannot mark it done yourself. |

## Emails you may get

- **A reminder.** Your training officer can send you a list of your open items. Its subject starts "Action needed".
- **A class assignment.** "You have been assigned a new class", when a requirement assigns you a class.
- **A renewal notice.** "Your National Registry certification was renewed", when Prodigy finds a later expiration date at the National Registry. It asks you to confirm the issue date on your certifications list.

## What you will not see

- Other people's requirements.
- Any screening your organization runs.
- The panel itself, if you have an individual account or your organization has not turned this feature on.
