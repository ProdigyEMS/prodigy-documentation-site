**Pulse > Overview** answers one question: where do your providers stand today. It has no table of people. Every count on it opens the Roster, filtered to the rows that count is made of. {% .lead %}

In the organization view the page covers the whole organization. In a department view it covers that department. Counts include active members only.

---

## Count cards

A card with a count above zero is tinted. Click any card to open the Roster with that card's filters.

Each card counts Roster rows, one for each provider and requirement. A provider who is missing two required certifications counts twice.

| Card | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| Expired or suspended | Certifications that expired in the last 180 days, plus every suspended or revoked license |
| Expiring within 90 days | Certifications expiring in the next 90 days. The line under it gives the number inside 30 days. |
| Missing required certifications | Certification requirements for which a provider has nothing on file |
| Classes overdue | Class requirements past due. The line under it gives the number due soon. |
| Series overdue | Providers with a series session past its days to complete |
| Sign-offs needed | Administrative and Skill items that are Missing or Overdue |
| Assignments overdue | Class and training plan assignments past their due date |

Snoozed items are left out of every card except **Assignments overdue**, because an assignment cannot be snoozed.

## What needs your attention

This list has one row for each requirement with open gaps. A row shows how many providers are overdue, missing and due soon, the requirement's owner, and how many of its follow-ups are assigned to you. Click a row to open the Roster filtered to that requirement.

The first five requirements are shown. **Show all** lists the rest.

Under the requirements, the list can also show:

- **Screening matches awaiting review**, for organization Admins, with a **Review screening** button.
- **N providers aren't covered by any requirement yet**, with a **Define a requirement** button. These are active members that no requirement applies to, so nothing about them is being tracked.
- **Waiting on you**: follow-ups assigned to you. Click one to open it.

When nothing is overdue, missing or waiting on you, the list reads **All clear in this scope**.

## Compliance trend

The chart plots four counts over the last 12 months: **Expired or suspended**, **Missing required**, **Classes overdue** and **Series overdue**.

Pulse captures the counts once a month, on the 1st at 9:00 a.m. Eastern. The chart draws a line once two captures exist, so a new organization sees it after its second 1st of the month. There is no button to capture a point on demand.

The monthly capture is taken for the organization as a whole. In a department view the chart stays empty.

## The setup checklist

**Get Pulse set up** sits above the cards until every step is done or you dismiss it. See [Set Up Pulse](/docs/pulse/setup).

## What the Overview does not have

- No department picker. To look at one department from the organization view, open the Roster and use its **Department** filter.
- No reminders and no export. Both are on the [Roster](/docs/pulse/roster).
