title: Who Can Do What
description: What a provider, a department Training Officer, an organization Training Officer and an organization Admin can each see and do in Pulse.

What you can do in Pulse depends on your role and on where you hold it. A role held on the organization reaches every department. A role held on one department reaches that department only. {% .lead %}

---

## The four positions

| Position | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Provider | A General User. No Training Officer or Admin role anywhere. |
| Department Training Officer | Training Officer or Admin on a department, and a General User on the organization |
| Organization Training Officer | Training Officer on the organization |
| Organization Admin | Admin on the organization |

When someone holds a role at both levels, the higher one applies in that department.

## What each can do

| | Provider | Department Training Officer | Organization Training Officer | Organization Admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See their own My requirements | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Open Overview, Roster, Matrix | No | Their department | Yes | Yes |
| Create and change requirements | No | Their department's | Yes | Yes |
| Manage groups, rules and suggestions | No | Their department's | Yes | Yes |
| Send reminders | No | Their department | Yes | Yes |
| Assign a follow-up, comment | No | Their department | Yes | Yes |
| Sign off | No | No | Yes, unless the requirement names other people | Yes, unless the requirement names other people |
| Snooze | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Set employment dates | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Create and change programs | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Verify NREMT certifications | Their own | Their department | Yes | Yes |
| Set up the digest and the report | No | Their department's | Yes | Yes |
| Receive the digest and the report | No | If added | If added | If added |
| Run the Audit Ready Export | No | Their department | Yes | Yes |
| Open Screening, review matches | No | No | No | Yes |
| Create API tokens | No | No | No | Yes |
| Set organization notification rules | No | No | No | Yes |

## Why some actions need an organization role

Sign-offs, snoozes and employment dates are saved for the organization, not for a department. They need a Training Officer or Admin role held on the organization, whichever view you are in.

A department Training Officer can still own a requirement, be assigned a follow-up, comment, and send reminders.

## Screening

Only an organization Admin, in the organization view, can open the Screening page, run a screening, review a match or export the evidence. Training Officers receive the number of pending matches in the weekly digest and the monthly report, and see **Pending review** or **Excluded** against a provider on the Roster.

## Providers

Providers cannot open any Pulse page. They see their own items, are never shown screening, and cannot see anyone else. See [My Requirements](/docs/pulse/my-requirements).
