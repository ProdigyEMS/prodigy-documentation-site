**Pulse > Matrix** is a grid with a row for each provider and a column for each requirement. Use it to see a pattern across a requirement or across a person, and to act on a whole column at once. {% .lead %}

---

## Read the grid

- Each column header shows the requirement's compliance percentage and its number of gaps.
- The last column, **Overall**, is each provider's most severe status across requirements. Exclusion screening is not part of the Matrix. A pending or confirmed match shows on the Roster, to organization Admins.
- A cell shows the status by color. A late item reads as days late, an upcoming one as days left, and a continuous series as attended of owed, such as 9/12.
- **Doesn't apply** marks a requirement that does not apply to that provider.

The Matrix opens with **Issues only** on, so providers with nothing open are hidden. Turn it off to see everyone. **Include people no longer active** adds gray rows for people who left.

Up to 200 providers are shown on one page. Past that, the grid is paged.

Assignments are not columns on the Matrix. They appear on the Roster only.

## Open a cell

- Click a **Compliant** cell to preview the certificate or evidence, with **Open full size**. The preview has no download button.
- Click any other cell that applies to open the [gap page](/docs/pulse/gaps).

## Act on a column

Open the menu in a column's header.

- **Assign training to providers with gaps** is offered on Class columns. It opens the assignment form with the class and those providers filled in.
- **Send reminder to providers with gaps** emails each provider with a gap in that column. The same rules apply as for [Roster reminders](/docs/pulse/roster#send-reminders).

## Filter the columns

**Filters** lets you choose which requirements appear as columns. A program's **Matrix view** button opens the Matrix with only that program's requirements, shown as a chip you can remove.

## Save a view

A saved view remembers a set of requirements and item types under a name.

1. Set the filters you want.
2. Click **Save current as view** and name it.

A saved view is shared with every Training Officer and Admin who uses the Matrix in your organization. In the organization view, anyone can delete any view. In a department view, you can delete only your own. A view cannot be renamed.

## Export

The **Export** menu offers **Export matrix CSV** and the **Audit Ready Export**. See [Exports](/docs/pulse/exports).
